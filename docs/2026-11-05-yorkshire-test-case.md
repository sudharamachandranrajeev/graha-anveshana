## Letting rules and council notes

- **Leeds (Headingley)**: Leeds has selective licensing in parts of six wards (Armley, Beeston and Holbeck, Burmantofts and Richmond Hill, Hunslet and Riverside, Gipton and Harehills, Farnley and Wortley). Headingley & Hyde Park is not one of them. https://www.leeds.gov.uk/housing/information-for-landlords/selective-licensing
- **Leeds (Headingley)**: Social housing in Leeds is let through Leeds Homes (choice-based lettings). https://leeds.gov.uk/housing/finding-a-home/lettings-policy
- **Leeds (Seacroft)**: Leeds has selective licensing in parts of six wards (Armley, Beeston and Holbeck, Burmantofts and Richmond Hill, Hunslet and Riverside, Gipton and Harehills, Farnley and Wortley). Killingbeck & Seacroft is not one of them. https://www.leeds.gov.uk/housing/information-for-landlords/selective-licensing
- **Leeds (Seacroft)**: Social housing in Leeds is let through Leeds Homes (choice-based lettings). https://leeds.gov.uk/housing/finding-a-home/lettings-policy
- **Pontefract**: No selective licensing found in Wakefield district. An Article 4 Direction on small shared houses (HMOs) covers parts of Castleford and Wakefield city, so check before converting a house to a shared home. Pontefract is not named. https://www.wakefield.gov.uk/planning/planning-applications-and-advice/help-with-a-planning-application/article-4-direction-for-smaller-houses-in-multiple-occupation
- **Pontefract**: Social housing here is let through Vico Homesearch (choice-based lettings). https://www.wakefield.gov.uk/housing/finding-a-new-home/what-is-choice-based-lettings
- **Castleford**: No selective licensing found in Wakefield district. An Article 4 Direction on small shared houses (HMOs) covers parts of Castleford and Wakefield city, so check before converting a house to a shared home. Pontefract is not named. https://www.wakefield.gov.uk/planning/planning-applications-and-advice/help-with-a-planning-application/article-4-direction-for-smaller-houses-in-multiple-occupation
- **Castleford**: Social housing here is let through Vico Homesearch (choice-based lettings). https://www.wakefield.gov.uk/housing/finding-a-new-home/what-is-choice-based-lettings
- **Knottingley**: No selective licensing found in Wakefield district. An Article 4 Direction on small shared houses (HMOs) covers parts of Castleford and Wakefield city, so check before converting a house to a shared home. Pontefract is not named. https://www.wakefield.gov.uk/planning/planning-applications-and-advice/help-with-a-planning-application/article-4-direction-for-smaller-houses-in-multiple-occupation
- **Knottingley**: Social housing here is let through Vico Homesearch (choice-based lettings). https://www.wakefield.gov.uk/housing/finding-a-new-home/what-is-choice-based-lettings
- **Doncaster (Hexthorpe)**: Selective licensing applies in Hexthorpe (scheme 2022 to 2027). Landlords need a licence. https://www.doncaster.gov.uk/services/business-investment/selective-licensing
- **Doncaster (Hexthorpe)**: Social housing is let through Doncaster HomeChoice (St Leger Homes). You need a local connection. https://stlegerhomes.co.uk/find-a-home/doncaster-homechoice/allocations
- **Doncaster (Rossington)**: Doncaster's selective licensing covers Hexthorpe only. Edlington's ended in 2023. https://www.doncaster.gov.uk/services/business-investment/selective-licensing
- **Doncaster (Rossington)**: Social housing is let through Doncaster HomeChoice (St Leger Homes). You need a local connection. https://stlegerhomes.co.uk/find-a-home/doncaster-homechoice/allocations
- **Rotherham**: Rotherham has selective licensing in six areas from February 2026 (town centre, Masbrough and Kimberworth, Thurcroft, Dinnington, Brinsworth, Parkgate). Check whether the street is in one. https://www.rotherham.gov.uk/housing/selective-licensing-2026-2031
